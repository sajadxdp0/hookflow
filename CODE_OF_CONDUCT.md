# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

HookFlow is a project where people should be able to contribute without harassment or discrimination. We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically focused community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials or customer data.
- Stay on topic and help maintain a welcoming environment for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Advocating or encouraging any of the behavior above.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/sajadxdp0/hookflow/security/advisories/new). For an urgent security issue, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include enough context for the maintainers to understand and investigate the concern; do not include unrelated sensitive information.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporter privacy as far as practical. They may remove inappropriate content and take proportionate action, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal of contributions or revocation of repository access.

This code is adapted to the HookFlow project and is not a statement of legal rights or guarantees.
